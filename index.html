Our Story

A Parisian institution
since 1976

Brioche Dorée was born in Brittany with a simple belief — that quality food should be accessible to everyone, every day. Today, Strada Café is our love letter to the Parisian canteen.

Every dish honours the season. Every coffee is pulled with care. Every table invites you to stay a little longer.
